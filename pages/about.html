About HandyHire

What is HandyHire?

HandyHire is a service marketplace that connects customers with on-demand service professionals. Whether you need a plumber, electrician, carpenter, cleaner, or another skilled worker, HandyHire helps you find and book the right person for the job.

Our Purpose

The purpose of HandyHire is to make it simple for customers to find trusted service providers and for skilled workers to reach more customers. The platform brings both sides together in one place, so bookings can be arranged through a clear, structured process.

How the Marketplace Works

HandyHire works as a two-sided marketplace. Customers browse available professionals, review their profiles, and submit a booking. Service providers receive booking requests, review the job details, and accept the work they can complete. The booking then moves through a shared status flow so both sides stay informed.

Benefits for Customers

  • Browse and search for professionals by skill, location, and price.
  • Review provider profiles before booking.
  • Submit bookings and track their status through the app.
  • Confirm completion and leave a review after the job.

Benefits for Providers

  • Create a professional profile to showcase skills and services.
  • Receive booking requests from customers.
  • Accept the jobs that fit your availability.
  • Build a reputation through customer reviews.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make household and professional services easier to find, book, and complete for everyone.
