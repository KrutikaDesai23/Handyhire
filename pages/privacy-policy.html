Privacy Policy

Draft / MVP Information

This page describes the intended privacy practices for the current HandyHire project. It is not a finalized legal privacy policy.

Privacy Policy

Information We Handle

HandyHire handles information needed to provide its platform functionality. This includes account information, profile information, booking information, and photos uploaded for jobs.

Account and Profile Information

When you create an account, we store the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, and any profile information you choose to add. Authentication information is used to let you sign in to your account.

Booking Information

Booking details, including the job description, schedule, and any photos you upload, are used to arrange and complete the service between you and the other party.

Sharing Information for a Booking

When a booking is arranged, the information needed to complete that booking is shared between the customer and the provider. This is limited to what is required for the service to take place.

Data Security

Protecting your information is a general product goal. We aim to handle your data responsibly and to keep it secure.

Your Responsibility

Please avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information through the platform. Only provide the details needed to arrange and complete a booking.
