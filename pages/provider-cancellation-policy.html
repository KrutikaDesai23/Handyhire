Cancellation Policy

Draft / MVP Information

This page describes the currently implemented cancellation behavior for the HandyHire project. It is not a finalized legal cancellation policy.

Cancellation Policy

Current Behavior

In the current version of HandyHire, a booking can be cancelled while it is in the Accepted status. Once a booking has been cancelled, it is considered final and cannot be changed further.

When Cancellation Is Not Available

Cancellation is not available once a booking has moved past the Accepted status. A booking that is In Progress, has requested completion, or is Completed cannot be cancelled.

No Fees or Deadlines

There are currently no cancellation fees or fixed cancellation deadlines. The ability to cancel depends on the current status of the booking.

Before Official Launch

Final cancellation and rescheduling policies will be finalized before the service officially launches.
