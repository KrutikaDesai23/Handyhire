How It Works for Providers

Getting Started

  1. Create your provider profile.
  2. Add your services and profile information.
  3. Receive booking requests from customers.
  4. Review the customer and job details.
  5. Accept the request if you can complete the work.
  6. Start the job when you begin the work.
  7. Request completion when the job is done.
  8. The customer confirms the completion.
  9. Receive a review from the customer.

Booking Statuses

Bookings move through a clear status flow:

  • Pending - a customer has submitted a booking and is waiting for your response.
  • Accepted - you have accepted the booking.
  • In Progress - you have started the job.
  • Completion Requested - you have finished the work and requested confirmation.
  • Completed - the customer has confirmed the job is complete.
