Safety Guidance

For Customers

  • Review the information available on a provider profile before booking.
  • Clearly describe the work you need so the provider understands the job.
  • Use the platform's booking and status flow to manage the job.
  • Avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Report suspicious behavior through the available support channels.

For Providers

  • Review the job details before accepting a booking.
  • Communicate clearly with the customer about the work.
  • Follow appropriate workplace safety practices.
  • Do not request unnecessary customer information.
  • Report suspicious activity through the available support channels.

A Note on Verification

Users should review the information available on a provider profile before booking. HandyHire does not currently perform background checks, KYC verification, or official vetting of providers. Please use your own judgment and the information shown on each profile when deciding who to book.
