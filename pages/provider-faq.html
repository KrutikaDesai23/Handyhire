FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I receive booking requests?

When a customer submits a booking, it appears in your Activity section as a pending request. You can review the job details there.

How do I accept a booking?

From the Activity section, open the pending booking and choose to accept it. The booking then moves to the Accepted status.

What does Start Job do?

When you begin the work, you can start the job. This moves the booking to the In Progress status so the customer knows the work has started.

How do I request completion?

When the job is finished, you can request completion. The customer then confirms the work is complete, which moves the booking to the Completed status.

How do team bookings work?

Team bookings let a customer hire a coordinated team for a bigger job. The team is booked as a whole, and the booking follows the same status flow as individual bookings.

Can customers add job photos?

Yes. When a customer submits a booking, they can add photos of the job. These photos help you understand the work required before you accept the booking.

How do I update my profile?

You can update your professional profile from the Profile section, including your services, availability, and other information.
