How It Works for Customers

Booking a Service

  1. Browse available workers on the home screen.
  2. Search or filter based on your requirements, such as profession, location, or price.
  3. View a worker's profile to learn more about them.
  4. Choose the booking option that fits your needs.
  5. Submit your booking with the job details.
  6. The worker reviews and accepts your booking.
  7. Track the booking status as it moves through the flow.
  8. The worker starts the job.
  9. The worker requests completion when the work is done.
  10. You confirm the completion of the job.
  11. Leave a review for the worker.

Booking Statuses

Bookings move through a clear status flow:

  • Pending - your booking has been submitted and is waiting for the worker to respond.
  • Accepted - the worker has accepted your booking.
  • In Progress - the worker has started the job.
  • Completion Requested - the worker has finished the work and requested confirmation.
  • Completed - you have confirmed the job is complete.
