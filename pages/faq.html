FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a worker?

Browse the available workers on the home screen, view a worker's profile, and submit a booking with your job details. The worker will then review and accept your booking.

Can I search for a specific profession?

Yes. You can search by name, skill, or location, and use the filter options to narrow down the list of available professionals.

Can I filter workers by price?

Yes. You can sort the list of workers by price, from low to high or high to low, to find a professional that fits your budget.

What happens after I submit a booking?

Your booking is submitted as pending. The worker reviews the job details and can accept it. You can track the status of your booking in the Activity section.

What do the booking statuses mean?

Bookings move through these statuses: Pending, Accepted, In Progress, Completion Requested, and Completed. See the How It Works page for a full explanation of each status.

Can I cancel a booking?

Cancellation is handled through the booking flow. The ability to cancel depends on the current status of the booking. See the Cancellation Policy page for more information.

Can I book a team or a package?

Yes. You can browse multitasking and team packages from the home screen. A team package lets you hire a coordinated team for a bigger job, and the booking follows the same status flow as an individual booking.

Can I add job photos?

Yes. When submitting a booking, you can add photos of the job so the professional can understand the work required.

How does completion confirmation work?

When the worker finishes the job, they request completion. You then confirm that the job is complete, which moves the booking to the Completed status.

How do I review a worker?

After a booking is completed, you can leave a review for the worker from the Activity section.
